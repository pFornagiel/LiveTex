\section{Introduction}
Overleaf and the Typst web app made a tight edit--preview loop the norm.
This extension brings the same loop into the editor: the PDF on the right
is recompiled from your \emph{unsaved} buffers a moment after you stop typing,
and a double-click on the PDF jumps back to the source line.

\subsection{Why Tectonic?}
Tectonic is a self-contained \TeX{} engine. It needs no system \TeX{} Live
installation and downloads only the packages a document actually uses.
